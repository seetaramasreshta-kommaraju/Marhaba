Our Story

Welcome to Marhaba Indo Arabic Restaurant, Vijayawada's premier destination for authentic Arabian and Indian fusion dining. Nestled in Kanuru on Bandar Road, Tadigadapa, we bring the bold flavours of the Middle East together with the rich culinary heritage of India.

From slow-cooked Mandi rice to perfectly charred Tandoori Chicken and flame-grilled kebabs, every dish at Marhaba is crafted with the finest ingredients and traditional techniques. We are proud to be one of the best-rated Arabian restaurants in Vijayawada, with a 4.8-star Google rating.
