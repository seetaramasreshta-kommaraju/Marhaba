Our Story

Welcome to Marhaba Indo Arabic Restaurant, where rich culinary traditions meet unforgettable taste. Located in the heart of Kanuru on Bandar Road, Tadigadapa, Vijayawada, we bring together the bold flavours of Indian cuisine and the aromatic delights of Arabic cooking.

Our chefs prepare every dish with passion and the finest halal-certified ingredients — from authentic slow-cooked Mandi to flame-grilled Tandoori Chicken, aromatic kebabs, and traditional Kunafa desserts. We believe great food tells a story, and at Marhaba, that story spans two rich culinary cultures.

Whether you are joining us for a family feast, a celebration, or a quiet weekday dinner, our warm hospitality and comfortable dining space in Vijayawada make every visit special. Our guests consistently rate us 4.8 stars on Google — a testament to our commitment to quality.
